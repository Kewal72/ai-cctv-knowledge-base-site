    Handling customer objections¶

    Draft

    Last updated: 2026-10-11

    In one line¶

    An objection is a question in disguise. Listen, agree with the feeling, ask for the real reason, answer simply with proof, check, and always end with a small next step.

    Why it matters to the customer¶

    Answering concerns honestly, with proof, helps customers make a confident decision, and helps us win the sale without cutting the price.

    The 5-step method¶

    flowchart LR
    A["1. Listen<br/>don't interrupt"] --> B["2. Agree<br/>with the feeling"]
    B --> C["3. Ask<br/>the real reason"]
    C --> D["4. Answer<br/>+ proof"]
    D --> E["5. Check<br/>'Does that help?'"]
    E --> F["Next step:<br/>survey, demo, quote"]

    Proof beats talk: the live demo, spec sheets, our test log, the payback calculation, real examples.

    Money objections¶

    Objection What they really mean Answer Proof
    "Too expensive." "I don't see the value yet" "What does a night guard or one theft cost you? This pays for itself in about X months." Then offer Basic Payback calculation, 3 packages
    "Online is cheaper." "Why pay more for the same thing?" "Many cheap ones are normal motion detection with an 'AI' sticker. Ours includes setup, AI tuning and local service." Two spec sheets side by side
    "Why pay for AMC every year?" "Will I get anything for it?" "AI cameras need tuning, cleaning and updates. We visit every 3 months, so it works when you need it." AMC checklist

    "We don't need it" objections¶

    Objection What they really mean Answer Proof
    "My current CCTV is fine." "I don't see a problem" "Does it warn you while something happens, or do you see it the next day? You can keep your cameras; we just upgrade the brain." Upgrade routes
    "Motion detection is enough." "I don't see the difference" "How many motion alerts do you get at night? Do you still check them all?" Demo: 24-hour alert count
    "I have a guard." "I'm already covered" "AI helps him. One guard can't watch every wall at 3am, but the camera can, and it alerts him." Siren + phone alert demo

    Doubt and trust objections¶

    Objection What they really mean Answer Proof
    "AI doesn't work / gives false alarms." "I don't want to waste money" "True if set up badly. We do a site visit, place it properly, and fine-tune for 2 weeks." Plus one honest limit Test log, 2-week tuning promise
    "What if the internet or power goes off?" "Will it fail when I need it?" "AI and recording work on site without internet; only phone alerts need it. We add a UPS for power cuts." Edge AI / AI NVR work offline
    "Chinese brand, is my data safe?" Security and government worries "We can use Indian brands such as CP Plus or Matrix, and keep the system secure with strong passwords and updates." Brand options
    "Is face recognition legal?" Privacy risk "We do it properly: signage, staff consent, secure storage. Or we skip face recognition and use person alerts only." DPDP basics

    Delay and difficulty objections¶

    Objection What they really mean Answer Proof
    "I'll think about it." Something's unclear, or not a priority "What are you unsure about: price, features or timing?" Answer that one thing Free site survey or demo visit
    "It's too complicated." Afraid of technology "We set it all up. You'll just get an alert with a video when something important happens." Live phone demo

    Never do these¶

    Don't Why
    Argue with the customer You win the argument, lose the sale
    Criticise competitors by name Unprofessional; compare features, not people
    Promise "100%" or "no theft ever" Breaks trust when something goes wrong
    Drop the price immediately Remove items instead
    Make up answers Say "Let me check and get back to you today," and do it

    Always end with a next step¶

    • "Let me do a free site survey, no obligation."
    • "Come and see the live demo at our shop. It takes 5 minutes."
    • "I'll send you 3 package options by tomorrow."
    • "Can I call you on Friday after you've discussed it?"

    Example¶

    A warehouse owner said our price was too high because his cousin bought cameras online for half the price. We asked how many alerts the cousin got at night ("many, he switched them off"), showed our 24-hour test (many motion alerts vs a few real AI alerts), offered the Basic package that keeps existing cameras, and booked a free site survey for Saturday.

    How to explain it to a customer¶

    "That's a fair question. Let me show you rather than just tell you."
