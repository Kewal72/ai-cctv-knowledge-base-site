    AI product lines by brand¶

    Draft

    Last updated: 2026-10-10

    Check with the distributor

    Brands rename and update product lines often, and prices change. Anything marked (to verify) must be confirmed with our distributor, who is the final source for models, prices, stock and certification.

    In one line¶

    Every brand has entry, advanced and premium AI tiers. Hikvision and Dahua are the most common; CP Plus, Matrix and Godrej are strong Indian options, often required for government work.

    Why it matters to the customer¶

    The right brand and tier gives the customer the features they need at the right price, with local service and no compliance problems.

    The 3 tiers¶

    Tier What it does Price Typical customer
    Entry AI Person and vehicle filter, intrusion, line-crossing ₹ Shops, homes, small warehouses
    Advanced AI Face recognition, ANPR, people counting, smart search ₹₹ Showrooms, societies, factories, offices
    Premium / enterprise Many AI features at once, large systems, integrations ₹₹₹ Big campuses, hospitals, government, large retail

    Hikvision (China)¶

    Product line What it is
    AcuSense Entry AI: person and vehicle filtering on cameras, DVRs and NVRs
    ColorVu Colour video at night. Not AI by itself; often combined with AcuSense
    DeepinView Advanced AI cameras: face recognition, ANPR, counting
    DeepinMind AI NVRs with face recognition and smart search
    Turbo HD Analog range, including AcuSense DVRs (useful for upgrades)

    Dahua (China)¶

    Product line What it is
    WizSense Entry AI: person and vehicle detection, perimeter protection, smart motion detection
    WizMind Advanced AI: face recognition, people counting, ANPR, detailed search
    Full-colour Colour night vision
    TiOC Active deterrence cameras with red-and-blue light and siren
    XVR Hybrid recorders for analog cameras, many with AI

    CP Plus (India)¶

    • One of the biggest sellers in India, with a strong distributor and service network.
    • AI cameras and recorders with person and vehicle detection and more. Exact AI product line names (to verify).
    • A strong choice when customers prefer an Indian brand or local service.

    Other brands¶

    Brand Position Note
    Uniview (UNV), China Mid-range Good value. AI range names (to verify)
    Matrix (Matrix Comsec), India Mid to enterprise Strong in enterprise and government projects
    Godrej Security, India Home to enterprise Trusted Indian name
    Honeywell, Bosch Premium Big corporates, large projects
    Axis (Sweden), Hanwha Wisenet (Korea) Premium High quality, high price; enterprise and critical sites

    Rules and restrictions¶

    Issue What it means
    Government and sensitive projects Often restrict Chinese brands. Indian brands (CP Plus, Matrix, Godrej) are preferred
    India's CCTV certification rules Cameras must pass security testing and certification before sale. Current status and approved models (to verify with distributor)
    "Make in India" preference Some customers, and government buyers, prefer Indian-made products

    Matching brands to customers¶

    Customer Good fit Why
    Small shop or home, tight budget Entry AI: CP Plus, Hikvision AcuSense, Dahua WizSense Low cost, reliable alerts
    Warehouse needing night deterrence Dahua TiOC, or Hikvision cameras with siren and light Detection + siren + light
    Housing society: ANPR + face at gate Hikvision DeepinView, Dahua WizMind, or CP Plus advanced (to verify) Needs dedicated ANPR and face cameras
    Government office or school Matrix, CP Plus, Godrej Indian brands, often required in tenders
    Large corporate campus Axis, Hanwha, Bosch, Honeywell, or enterprise ranges Premium quality, integrations, support

    Practical tips¶

    1. Stick to 2–3 brands our distributors stock well.
    2. Keep camera and NVR the same brand when possible, so AI works fully.
    3. Check warranty and local service in the city.
    4. Get the distributor's current AI price list (entry vs advanced).
    5. Don't trust the line name alone; check the spec sheet for actual AI features.

    How to explain it to a customer¶

    "All the big brands have basic and advanced AI ranges. Based on what you need and your budget, we'll suggest the right one. For government or sensitive sites, we use Indian brands that meet the rules."
