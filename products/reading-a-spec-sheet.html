    Reading a spec sheet¶

    Draft

    Last updated: 2026-10-10

    In one line¶

    Don't trust "AI" in the product name. Read the smart features section of the spec sheet: real AI says "deep learning" or "human/vehicle classification."

    Why it matters to the customer¶

    It stops us selling a product that won't do what the customer needs, and it explains why our price is higher than cheap "AI" cameras online.

    What's in a spec sheet¶

    Section What it tells you Look for
    Camera Image quality Resolution, lens, night vision range
    Smart / AI features ("Smart event", "Deep learning", "VCA") What AI it actually does The most important section
    Video Recording format H.265
    Network Compatibility ONVIF
    Interface Extra hardware Built-in speaker, light, alarm input/output
    General Build and power IP67, PoE, operating temperature

    Real AI vs marketing label¶

    Real AI says… Marketing label says…
    "Deep learning" "Smart", "intelligent" (no detail)
    "Human and vehicle classification" "Motion detection" or "smart motion detection" with no classification
    "Line-crossing / intrusion based on human/vehicle" "Line-crossing, intrusion" (could be motion-based)
    "False alarm filtering (human/vehicle)" "AI" in the product name only
    "Face recognition, face library of X faces" "Face detection" or "face capture"
    "ANPR / LPR" with stated conditions "Vehicle detection" 
    flowchart TD
    Q1{"Says deep learning or<br/>human/vehicle classification?"}
    Q1 -- No --> N["Assume NOT real AI<br/>until proven"]
    Q1 -- Yes --> Q2{"Works alone, or only with<br/>a compatible NVR?"}
    Q2 -- Alone --> Y[Real AI camera]
    Q2 -- "NVR only" --> C["Real AI, but only with<br/>that brand's AI NVR"]

    Traps in the small print¶

    Trap What it really means
    "Supported with compatible NVR" Works only with that brand's AI NVR
    "Face capture" Only takes face photos; recognition needs an AI NVR or software
    Recorder: "AI: 4-ch", "Perimeter protection: 8-ch" AI works on only that many cameras
    "Up to" distances Ideal conditions; plan for less
    "Requires license" Extra payment to unlock the feature
    ANPR "speed ≤ X km/h", "angle ≤ X°" Plates are read only within these conditions

    Example: two cameras sold as "AI"¶

    Camera A: "AI Smart Camera" Camera B: "4MP Bullet Camera"
    Smart features listed Smart motion detection, line crossing, intrusion, tampering Deep learning: human and vehicle classification, line crossing and intrusion (human/vehicle), false alarm filtering
    Actually AI? Probably not: no classification Yes
    At a site with trees and dogs Many false alarms Few false alarms

    Other specs worth knowing¶

    Spec Simple meaning Why it matters
    IR range How far night vision reaches AI can't detect beyond it at night
    Colour night vision Colour video at night using light Easier to identify clothes and vehicles
    WDR Handles bright and dark areas together Doorways with sunlight behind people
    H.265 Video compression Saves hard disk space
    IP67 Dust- and water-proof Outdoor cameras
    IK10 Vandal-proof Low-mounted or public places
    Built-in speaker / light Warnings and flashing Active deterrence

    The 6-question check¶

    • [ ] Says human/vehicle classification or deep learning?
    • [ ] Which AI features exactly?
    • [ ] Works alone, or only with a compatible NVR?
    • [ ] Recorders: how many channels get AI?
    • [ ] Any extra licence needed?
    • [ ] Night vision range enough for the site?

    Example¶

    A customer asked why our AI camera costs more than an "AI camera" online. The online camera's spec sheet listed smart motion detection with no classification. Ours listed deep learning, human/vehicle classification, false alarm filtering and a built-in siren. Showing both spec sheets justified the price.

    How to explain it to a customer¶

    "Many cheap cameras just put an 'AI' sticker on normal motion detection, so trees and dogs keep setting them off. Ours actually recognises people and vehicles. You'll get alerts you can trust. I can show you both spec sheets."
