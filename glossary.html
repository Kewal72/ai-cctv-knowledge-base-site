    Glossary¶

    Draft

    Last updated: 2026-10-10

    Simple meanings of the technical terms used in this knowledge base, in A–Z order. Use the search box at the top to find a term quickly.

    Term Simple meaning Learn more
    4G camera A camera that sends video over a mobile SIM network. Used where there's no wired internet, e.g. construction sites. AI use cases by customer type
    Active deterrence Cameras with a built-in siren, speaker or flashing light that scare intruders away, not just record them. Intrusion, line-crossing and loitering
    AcuSense Hikvision's entry AI range: person and vehicle filtering on cameras and recorders. AI product lines
    AHD / HDCVI / HD-TVI Different types of analog HD camera technology. Different brands use different types. "Turbo HD" is one brand's name for HD-TVI. Adding AI to existing cameras
    AI box A separate device that reads video from existing cameras or recorders, runs AI and sends alerts. Nothing else needs replacing. Adding AI to existing cameras
    AI camera A camera with an AI chip inside that recognises people, vehicles and so on. Also called edge AI. What makes a camera "AI"
    AI chip A small processor inside a camera or recorder, built specially to run AI. What makes a camera "AI"
    AI model The program that recognises objects, trained on millions of photos. What makes a camera "AI"
    AI NVR An NVR (recorder for IP cameras) with an AI chip that adds AI to normal cameras. Where the AI runs
    Allow list A list of known people or vehicles (employees, residents, VIPs). A match can open a gate or send a welcome alert. Face recognition, ANPR
    AMC Annual Maintenance Contract. A yearly service fee the customer pays for maintenance and support. Where the AI runs
    Analog camera An older type of CCTV camera that uses coaxial cable and connects to a DVR. Adding AI to existing cameras
    ANPR / LPR Automatic Number Plate Recognition (also License Plate Recognition). The camera reads vehicle number plates automatically. ANPR
    Battery optimisation A phone setting that stops apps running in the background. A common reason CCTV alerts don't arrive. Why AI fails
    Block list A list of people or vehicles to watch out for (known shoplifters, banned vehicles). A match sends an alert. Face recognition, ANPR
    BOM Bill of Materials. The list of all items and quantities in a quote. Pricing, packages and AMC
    Boom barrier The motorised gate arm at a vehicle entrance. Can open automatically with ANPR. AI use cases by customer type
    Channel One camera input on a recorder. A 16-channel NVR can connect 16 cameras. AI may work on only some channels. Where the AI runs
    Cloud AI AI that runs on internet servers instead of on site. Usually has a monthly fee and needs good internet. Where the AI runs
    Coaxial cable The round cable with a metal pin at the end, used by analog cameras. Adding AI to existing cameras
    ColorVu Hikvision's colour night vision range. Not AI by itself; often combined with AcuSense. AI product lines
    Comprehensive AMC An AMC that covers service, labour and replacement parts. Pricing, packages and AMC
    Confidence score How sure the AI is about what it sees, e.g. "Person: 92%." How AI detects people and vehicles
    Conversion rate The share of visitors who buy. Bills ÷ visitors, e.g. 100 ÷ 500 = 20%. People counting
    Deep learning The type of AI used in modern cameras. On a spec sheet, a sign of real AI. Reading a spec sheet
    DeepinMind Hikvision's AI NVR range with face recognition and smart search. AI product lines
    DeepinView Hikvision's advanced AI camera range: face recognition, ANPR, counting. AI product lines
    Detection The AI finding an object and drawing a box around it, e.g. "a person is here." How AI detects people and vehicles
    Detection zone The area you draw on the camera screen where the AI should look. Leave out trees and roads to avoid false alarms. How AI detects people and vehicles
    DORI A standard for how much detail a camera captures at a distance: Detect, Observe, Recognise, Identify. Camera placement for AI
    DPDP Act India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. It protects personal data such as faces. Needs signage, consent and secure storage. Face recognition
    DVR Digital Video Recorder. Records video from analog cameras. Adding AI to existing cameras
    Edge AI AI that runs inside the camera itself ("at the edge"). Same as an AI camera. Where the AI runs
    Enrolment Uploading clear photos of people into a face recognition system. Face recognition
    Face capture The camera only takes photos of faces. Recognising who it is needs an AI NVR or software. Reading a spec sheet
    Face detection Knowing that a face is there, without knowing whose it is. Face recognition
    Face recognition Identifying who a person is by matching their face with an enrolled list. Face recognition
    Faceprint A number code made from the shape of a face, like a fingerprint for faces. Face recognition
    False alarm An alert for something that isn't a threat, e.g. a dog reported as a person. How AI detects people and vehicles
    Firmware The software built into a camera or recorder. Updates fix bugs and improve features. Why AI fails
    Footfall The number of people who visit a place. People counting
    Frame A single picture in a video. Video is many frames per second, like a flipbook. How AI detects people and vehicles
    H.265 A video compression format that saves hard disk space. Reading a spec sheet
    Heatmap A coloured map showing where people go or stand most. People counting
    HSRP High Security Registration Plate. India's standard number plate with a standard font, easier for ANPR to read. ANPR
    Hybrid system A system that combines AI cameras, an AI NVR and/or cloud, each doing a different job. Where the AI runs
    IK10 Vandal-proof rating: strong against hits. Reading a spec sheet
    Intrusion detection An alert when a person or vehicle enters a zone you've drawn. Intrusion, line-crossing and loitering
    IP camera A network camera that uses a LAN cable and connects to an NVR. Adding AI to existing cameras
    IP67 Dust- and water-proof rating, needed for outdoor cameras. Reading a spec sheet
    IR (infrared) Invisible light used for night vision. When IR fails, night images go dark. ANPR
    IR reflection IR light bouncing off a nearby wall, roof edge or pole, making the picture white or foggy. Camera placement for AI
    Lens (focal length) Sets how wide or far a camera sees. 2.8 mm = wide but short; 6–8 mm = narrow but further. Camera placement for AI
    Line-crossing An alert when a person or vehicle crosses a line you've drawn, in a chosen direction. Intrusion, line-crossing and loitering
    Loitering An alert when a person stays in a zone longer than a set time. Intrusion, line-crossing and loitering
    Missed event When the AI fails to alert for something real, e.g. an intruder. How AI detects people and vehicles
    Non-comprehensive AMC An AMC that covers service and labour; parts are charged extra. Pricing, packages and AMC
    NVR Network Video Recorder. Records video from IP cameras. Where the AI runs
    Occupancy How many people are inside right now (people in − people out). People counting
    OCR Optical Character Recognition. Reading text, such as a number plate, from an image. ANPR
    ONVIF A common "language" that lets cameras and recorders from different brands work together. Recording usually works across brands; AI features may not. Adding AI to existing cameras
    Overhead counting camera A ceiling camera looking straight down at an entrance, made for accurate people counting. People counting
    Payback period How many months a system takes to pay for itself: price ÷ monthly saving. Pricing, packages and AMC
    Pixels per metre How many tiny dots cover 1 metre of the scene. More = more detail. Camera placement for AI
    PoE Power over Ethernet. One network cable carries both video and power to an IP camera. Adding AI to existing cameras
    PPE detection AI that checks if workers wear safety gear (Personal Protective Equipment) such as helmets and vests. AI use cases by customer type
    Schedule The times when alerts are switched on, e.g. 10pm–7am only. Intrusion, line-crossing and loitering
    Sensitivity / threshold The setting that decides how sure the AI must be before alerting. Too high = false alarms; too low = missed events. How AI detects people and vehicles
    Shutter speed How fast the camera captures each picture. Fast shutter stops moving vehicles from looking blurry. ANPR
    Smart search Finding people or vehicles in recordings by colour, type and time, in minutes instead of hours. Smart search
    Solar camera A camera powered by a solar panel and battery. Used where there's no power supply. AI use cases by customer type
    Spec sheet / datasheet The PDF listing exactly what a product can do. Reading a spec sheet
    Stranger alert An alert when a face doesn't match anyone on the allow list. Face recognition
    TiOC Dahua's active deterrence cameras with red-and-blue light and siren. AI product lines
    UPS Uninterruptible Power Supply. A battery backup that keeps the recorder, router and switch running during power cuts. Why AI fails
    Varifocal lens A lens whose zoom can be adjusted at installation (motorised versions adjust remotely). Camera placement for AI
    VCA Video Content Analytics. A spec sheet heading for smart or AI features. Reading a spec sheet
    WDR Wide Dynamic Range. Handles bright and dark areas in the same picture, e.g. a sunny doorway. Reading a spec sheet
    WizMind Dahua's advanced AI range: face recognition, counting, ANPR, detailed search. AI product lines
    WizSense Dahua's entry AI range: person and vehicle detection, perimeter protection. AI product lines
    XVR A hybrid recorder that works with analog cameras and also some IP cameras. Many XVRs now include AI. Adding AI to existing cameras
