    Why AI fails, and how to fix it¶

    Draft

    Last updated: 2026-10-11

    In one line¶

    Most AI complaints aren't AI problems. Check in order: what the camera sees, the settings, the recorder, alert delivery to the phone, and only then the AI.

    Why it matters to the customer¶

    Fast, correct fixes keep customers happy and trusting AI CCTV. Regular maintenance prevents most problems.

    Troubleshooting order¶

    flowchart TD
    A["1. Live view, day and night:<br/>can a human clearly see the person?"] -- No --> A1["Camera, placement<br/>or light problem"]
    A -- Yes --> B["2. Settings: AI on, zone, line,<br/>direction, sensitivity, schedule"]
    B --> C["3. Recorder: AI channel limit,<br/>hard disk, date/time, firmware"]
    C --> D["4. Alert delivery: internet,<br/>app notifications, phone battery saver"]
    D --> E["5. Only then suspect the AI<br/>(contact distributor)"]

    "Too many false alarms"¶

    Likely cause Fix
    Sensitivity too high Lower it a little and test
    Zone includes trees, roads or neighbours Redraw the zone to cover only the property
    Spider webs or insects near the lens Clean regularly
    Rain drops on the lens Sunshade or hood; angle slightly down
    No schedule Alerts only for closed hours
    Plain motion detection on instead of AI Switch to human/vehicle detection
    Shadows, headlights or reflections Change the angle; remove the area from the zone

    "It missed someone"¶

    Likely cause Fix
    Person too far or small Check DORI range; add a camera or narrower lens
    Camera too high Lower to about 2.5–4 m
    Sensitivity too low Raise it a little and test
    Line at the edge of the picture Redraw where the person is full body
    Wrong line direction Set the direction to match the threat
    Outside the schedule Check schedule hours and recorder date/time
    AI off on that channel (channel limit) Move AI to the important cameras

    "Night is bad"¶

    Likely cause Fix
    Beyond the IR range Longer IR range, extra light, or colour night vision
    IR reflection Move away from walls and roof edges
    IR LEDs failed Replace the camera
    Headlight or streetlight glare Change the angle; WDR camera

    "Alerts aren't reaching my phone"¶

    The most common complaint, and often the AI is working fine.

    Likely cause Fix
    Phone battery saver stops the app (common on Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, Realme and others) Allow background running; turn off battery optimisation for the app
    App notifications off Turn on in the app and phone settings
    Internet down at the site Check the router (recording continues locally)
    Power cut UPS for recorder, router and PoE switch
    Event not set to "notify" Turn on app/cloud notification for that event
    Logged out or new phone Log in again; re-enable notifications

    "It suddenly stopped working"¶

    Likely cause Fix
    Settings changed or reset Re-check AI settings; keep a settings backup after installation
    Camera moved Re-aim and tighten the mount
    Hard disk failed or full Check disk health; replace
    Old firmware Update from the brand or distributor
    Wrong date and time Set the time zone and time sync

    "Face or plate reading is wrong"¶

    Likely cause Fix
    Face: bad angle, backlight, masks, poor enrolment photos See Face recognition
    ANPR: speed, angle, dirty or fancy plates, glare See ANPR

    Maintenance (AMC)¶

    When What
    First 1–2 weeks after installation Review alerts with the customer; fine-tune sensitivity and zones
    Every 3 months Clean lenses, remove webs, check angles, trim trees, check disk health
    Every 6 months Update firmware, check UPS battery, review schedules
    Season change Winter sun sits lower (new glare); monsoon brings rain and insects. Re-check angles

    Example¶

    A restaurant owner complained of no alerts for 3 days. The live view was clear, settings were correct, and the recorder log showed alerts were triggered. The real cause: a new phone whose battery saver blocked the app. Fixed in 2 minutes.

    How to explain it to a customer¶

    "AI cameras need some tuning and care, like any machine. In the first two weeks we fine-tune them for your site, and our AMC keeps them clean, updated and working properly."
